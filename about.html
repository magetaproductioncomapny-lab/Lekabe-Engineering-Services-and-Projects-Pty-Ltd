An emerging, wholly female-owned and black-owned engineering company serving mining, manufacturing and local municipalities across South Africa.

Our story

Technical strength with purpose.

Founded in 2015, Lekabe's facility is strategically located in Zinniaville, Rustenburg, close to the mining hub of the North West Province. The company also has a branch in Northam at 2366 Ext 6, Section, Northam, 0360.

With a Level 1 BBBEE rating, Lekabe has built a team dedicated to becoming a partner of choice in technical services and selected products. We develop lasting relationships by combining critical skills into custom-tailored, cost-effective and sustainable solutions.

GAOPALELWE L. PULE / FOUNDER & MD

The director

Gaopalelwe L. Pule

Gaopalelwe is a results-oriented artisan and one of the pioneering women to join the mining industry after 1994. She began at Anglo Platinum's Mogalakwena South Plant as an Apprentice Fitter, trained to Artisan Fitter, and rose to Mechanical Foreman at Anglo Platinum Mine's Western Limb Tailings Plant.

After leaving formal employment in 2017, she spearheaded Lekabe's establishment and developed the internal operational systems, policies and procedures needed to meet industry and client requirements.

Meet our team

People behind
the precision.

Motlagomang E. Mmope

Director and Shareholder

Motlagomang is a seasoned career administrator, with close to 30 years' experience serving in senior positions for organisations including the departments of Public Works, and Transport, Roads and Community Safety. An ardent supporter of gender equality and the development of women, she has been a major driving force behind the establishment and growth of Lekabe.

She has made vital contributions to the company's administrative procedures and remains an important sounding board for the Managing Director and Senior Management.

BM

Boitumelo J. Morweng

Senior Manager - Administration and Special Projects

With over 15 years serving in roles within the construction and mining sectors, Boitumelo holds a National Diploma in Building from Tshwane University of Technology and a National Certificate in Supervision of Construction Processes.

Her administrative training and extensive project experience make her central to the structuring and management of Lekabe's special projects.

JM

Joshua Muchechetere

Senior Manager - Operations

Joshua is a team player with strong leadership skills, enabling him to harness the collective energy and experience of Lekabe personnel in service of clients. With extensive construction and technology experience, he has served in senior positions and developed broad knowledge in civil and construction project management.

Driven by a commitment to the highest standards of execution, Joshua is an effective client communicator and is conversant with many software programs and applications. He holds a Bachelor of Science (Hons) degree in Land and Water Resources Engineering and is completing his thesis for a Master's Degree in Environmental Management, specialising in Environmental Water Requirements at North-West University, Potchefstroom Campus.
